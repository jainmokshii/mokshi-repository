Hi! I'm Mokshi Jain — a final-year Computer Science student specialising in AI & ML at VIT Bhopal (Batch of 2027) who loves creating things that make life a little easier.

My curiosity doesn't stop at code. You'll probably find me reading about space one day, debating sports the next, and somehow ending up discussing poetry, startups, or politics before dinner. I enjoy learning simply because it's interesting.

I started building MYNA at 20 — not because I knew it would succeed, but because I didn't want to spend years wondering, "What if?" That mindset shaped a PM internship at Thought Agile Ltd (UK) too, where I turned messy data into decisions stakeholders could act on.

I believe progress isn't made through perfect ideas. It's made through shipping, learning, failing better, and showing up again tomorrow.
